Upload reports for the same company and accounting period. Map the account allocation and report formulas below. Once reviewed, one input change propagates through those relationships. This creates scenario copies; it does not change accounting software or source bookings.

BWA = Betriebswirtschaftliche Auswertung (business performance report). SuSa = Summen- und Saldenliste (account totals and balances). KER = Kurzfristige Erfolgsrechnung (short-term profit report, often a BWA view). These are related reports from the books, not three interchangeable totals. VAT, receivables, credit/debit direction and account allocation require an explicit accounting rule.
